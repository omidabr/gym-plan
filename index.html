Three-Day Gym Plan

Monday upper · Wednesday lower · Friday upper-focused

Using the page at the gym: it opens on today's session. Tap an exercise to open it and fill in Today's log (kg × reps); last session's numbers are shown as hints and "Same as last time" copies them. Tapping ✓ or a circle marks the set done and starts the rest timer. History shows your progress chart. Your log is saved in this browser on this phone: no sign-in needed. Use Back up log (Week tab) now and then. ★ marks the exercises kept in the 45-minute version. The big ⏱ Timer button at the top opens a countdown or stopwatch for planks and other holds.

Muscle maps: red = main muscle, orange = strong helper, yellow = smaller role. The % shares are estimates from muscle-activity (EMG) and biomechanics research, meant to show which muscles do most of the work, not exact measurements. Tap Movement for start and finish positions, and 💪 MuscleWiki for a video and detailed how-to (needs internet).

Back and knee: your back is pain-free, so still brace and keep a neutral spine, especially on Romanian deadlifts and the leg press. Your knee sometimes hurts, so keep reps and depth pain-free and skip the leg extension on days it complains. Stop any exercise that brings on pain, and see a professional if it persists or gets worse.

Effort (RIR = reps in reserve): stop most sets with 1–3 good reps left. Last set of arm isolation: 0–1 left. Lunges, chin-ups and the new Romanian deadlift: 2–3 left. Rest: 90 s–2 min after big lifts, 75–90 s for mid-size lifts, about 60 s for isolation (45–60 s for hip machines and core). The timer starts at the short end; tap +30 s if you need more.

Day 1 · Monday · Upper
⏱ ~66 min23 working sets8 exercises
Chest · Back · Shoulders · Arms
🔥 Warm-up · about 10–12 min
  1. 5 min easy bike or rower.
  2. Plank about 2 min (two 1-min holds is fine).
  3. Side plank 45 s per side.
  4. Bench ramp-up: empty bar × 10, then about half your working weight × 5 (e.g. 20–25 kg). One light set of 10 before lat pulldown and before shoulder press.

Warm-up sets are not working sets: keep them easy.

Optional cardio · Tue / Thu / Sat

30–45 minutes easy, at a pace where you could hold a conversation: bike, rower, or incline walking. Choose knee-friendly options and keep it easy so it doesn't eat into recovery.

Missed a session?

Do the days in order (1 → 2 → 3) whatever the weekday, and don't squeeze two sessions into one. Keep at least one rest day between the two upper days (Day 1 and Day 3); Day 2 can sit next to either. Lost a whole week? Restart at Day 1 with the same weights. Short on time? Use the 45-minute version.

Week 1: find your working weights and write them down. After that, when you reach the top of the rep range on every set at the target RIR, add the smallest step (1–2.5 kg for dumbbells and upper body, 2.5–5 kg for leg machines) next session. The printed version has log columns for 4 weeks.
